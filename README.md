# TRACker — AI Food Scanner & Glycemic Nutrition Planner 🥑

**TRACker** is a production-grade AI food scanner, glycemic index sugar spike engine, budget Indian meal planner, medical report blood biomarker risk classifier, and zero-overrun nutrition platform built with React 18, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, and Vite.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **📸 AI Food & Barcode Scanner**
   - Live camera vision food recognition & barcode lookup for Indian & Global dishes.
   - Real-time nutritional breakdown (Calories, Protein, Carbs, Net Carbs, Fats, Fiber, Sugar, Glycemic Index & Load, NOVA ultra-processing group, Micronutrients).

2. **🛡️ Comprehensive Allergen & Dietary Safeguard Engine**
   - Automated conflict detection for Vegan, Jain, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, and Keto dietary restrictions.

3. **📊 Interactive Dashboard & Nutrient Analytics**
   - Calorie progress ring, macro target bars, hydration tracking, and weekly nutrition trend charts.

4. **🩺 Medical Report Biomarker Risk Classifier**
   - Analyzes blood test parameters (Fasting Glucose, HbA1c, Cholesterol, HDL, LDL, Triglycerides, Vitamin D3, B12, Hemoglobin) to score overall health and highlight deficiencies.

5. **🏋️ Personalized Workout & Supplement Engine**
   - Auto-generates Push/Pull/Legs exercise splits and evidence-based supplement protocols with safety checks.

6. **🛒 Grocery Planner & PDF Report Exporter**
   - Categorized grocery shopping lists and downloadable PDF health summaries.
